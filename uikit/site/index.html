FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our workflow, process and support.

How does your process work?

We start with research and strategy, followed by design, development and testing to ensure a smooth, high-performance delivery.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. We offer flexible support and maintenance plans tailored to your business needs.

Can I request custom features?

Absolutely. Every project is customized based on your goals, audience, and technical requirements.

How long does a typical project take?

Most branding and website projects take between 6–12 weeks depending on complexity, timeline, and revisions.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes! We frequently collaborate with new founders to help launch their first MVP, brand identity, and website experience.

What does your pricing include?

All pricing includes research, strategy, design exploration, revisions, delivery files, implementation support, and launch guidance.

Do you offer ongoing support or retainers?

Absolutely — many clients continue with us monthly for design, development, and growth support once the initial project is complete.
